# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes people of every background and level of experience.
Be considerate, constructive, and specific. Discuss evidence and implementation
without attacking people or assuming their political motives.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal insults, sexualised content,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in issues, pull requests
and other project spaces. Do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive conversations,
and restrict participation. Where appropriate, they will explain the decision
and allow a constructive correction; serious abuse may lead to immediate removal.
The same standards apply to maintainers.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for sensitive abuse reports. For a
non-sensitive moderation concern, open an issue describing the behavior without
repeating personal information. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.
Project moderation is best-effort; no response time is promised.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of affiliation with or adoption
of another community's enforcement programme.
